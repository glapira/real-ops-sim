# Standard Two-Crew Callouts

Adapted from common Boeing/Airbus two-crew philosophies for sim use. If you fly single-pilot on the sim, make the Pilot Monitoring (PM) callouts yourself, out loud. It sounds silly for the first five flights and then it becomes automatic and your scan gets dramatically better.

**PF** = Pilot Flying. **PM** = Pilot Monitoring.

---

## Before takeoff

| Event | Who calls | Callout |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Takeoff clearance received | PM | "Cleared for takeoff, runway [XX]" |
| Line up | PF | "Lining up, runway [XX]" |
| Thrust set | PF | "Set thrust" — PM confirms "Thrust set, [N1/EPR]" |

## Takeoff roll

| Speed / event | Who calls | Callout |
|---------------|-----------|---------|
| 80 kt | PM | "Eighty knots" — PF: "Checked" |
| V1 | PM | "V1" |
| VR | PM | "Rotate" |
| Positive climb | PM | "Positive climb" — PF: "Gear up" |
| Any anomaly below 80 kt | Either | "Stop" (memory: idle → reverse → brakes) |
| Any anomaly above 80 kt, below V1 | Either | Only "Stop" for engine failure, fire, windshear, or loss of control |

## Initial climb

| Event | Who calls | Callout |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Acceleration altitude | PM | "Acceleration altitude" — PF: "Flaps [next]" |
| Flap retraction schedule | PF | "Flaps [X]" — PM: "Flaps [X], speed checked" |
| Passing transition altitude | PM | "Transition altitude, set standard" |
| Level-off | PM | "One thousand to level" then "Level [FLxxx]" |

## Cruise

| Event | Who calls | Callout |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Frequency change | PM | Reads back, then "[Freq] set" |
| Altitude change | PM | "Cleared [FLxxx], set" — PF confirms |
| Deviation from cleared level > 100 ft | PM | "Altitude" |
| Fuel check every hour | PM | "Fuel check: [kg on board] vs [plan], [+/- kg]" |

## Descent

| Event | Who calls | Callout |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Top of descent | PM | "Top of descent" |
| Passing transition level | PM | "Transition level, set QNH [xxxx]" |
| 1000 ft to level | PM | "One thousand to level" |

## Approach

| Event | Who calls | Callout |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Localiser alive | PM | "Localiser alive" |
| Localiser captured | PM | "Localiser captured" |
| Glideslope alive | PM | "Glideslope alive" |
| Glideslope captured | PM | "Glideslope captured" |
| 1000 ft AAL | PM | "One thousand, stable" or "One thousand, not stable — go around" |
| 500 ft AAL | PM | "Five hundred, stable" (VMC gate) |
| 100 ft above minimums | PM | "One hundred above" |
| Minimums | PM | "Minimums" — PF: "Continue" (visual references) or "Go around, flaps [X]" |

## Landing

| Event | Who calls | Callout |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| 50 ft | PM | "Fifty" |
| 30 / 20 / 10 | PM | Radio altimeter callouts if fitted |
| Touchdown | PF | Fly the aircraft — do not stop flying at touchdown |
| Reverse | PF | "Reverse [green/normal]" — PM confirms |
| 80 kt (rollout) | PM | "Eighty knots" |
| 60 kt | PM | "Sixty knots" — PF: reverse to idle |
| Taxi speed | PF | "Vacating, [taxiway]" |

## Any abnormal

| Event | Who calls | Callout |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Master caution | PM | "Master caution, [system]" |
| Master warning | PM | "Master warning, [system]" |
| Memory items | PF | Calls each memory item as they do it |
| ECAM/QRH | PF | "ECAM actions" or "QRH, [checklist name]" |

---

## The point

Callouts are not ritual. They are a mechanism to force cross-checking between two pilots (or, on the sim, between the part of your brain flying and the part of your brain monitoring). Every callout is an opportunity to catch an error before it becomes an undesired aircraft state.

Say them out loud. Every time. That is the entire technique.
