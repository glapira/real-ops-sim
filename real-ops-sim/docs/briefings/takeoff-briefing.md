## Emergency plan — the important part

State each of these explicitly. Do not summarise.

**Before V1 (low-speed regime, below ~80 kt):**
Reject for any master caution, master warning, tyre failure, fire warning, or anything that clearly means the aircraft will not fly. Call **"STOP"**, close thrust levers, apply max reverse and brakes, maintain centreline, advise ATC.

**Before V1 (high-speed regime, above ~80 kt up to V1):**
Reject only for engine failure, fire warning, predictive windshear, or loss of directional control. Anything else, we go — the aircraft is safer airborne than trying to stop from high speed on the runway remaining.

**At or after V1:**
We are going flying. Continue takeoff, rotate normally at VR, target V2 to V2+15 as appropriate for the type, positive rate, gear up, follow the SID or the engine-out routing (see below).
